## 💡 What is the BMI Calculator?

The BMI Calculator helps you quickly understand whether your weight falls within a healthy range for your height. Body Mass Index (BMI) is a simple and widely used method to estimate weight categories and support better health awareness.

### What is BMI?
BMI is a numerical value calculated based on your weight and height. Healthcare professionals use it as a quick screening tool to identify potential weight-related health risks.

---

## 📐 How BMI is Calculated

**Formula:**
$$\text{BMI} = \frac{\text{weight (kg)}}{(\text{height in meters})^2}$$

**Example:**
If you weigh 70 kg and your height is 1.75 m:
$$\text{BMI} = \frac{70}{1.75 \times 1.75} = 22.9$$

---

## 📊 BMI Categories

- 🔹 **Underweight:** Less than 18.5
- 🟢 **Normal weight:** 18.5 – 24.9
- 🟠 **Overweight:** 25 – 29.9
- 🔴 **Obesity:** 30 or higher

---

## ❤️ Why BMI Matters

BMI provides a quick indication of whether your weight may increase the risk of health conditions such as heart disease, diabetes, or high blood pressure. It serves as a useful starting point for understanding your general health.

---

## ⚠️ Limitations of BMI

BMI does not measure body fat directly and does not take into account:
- Muscle mass
- Bone structure
- Body composition
- Age or gender differences
- Fitness level

*For example, athletes with high muscle mass may have a high BMI even if they have a low body fat percentage.*

---

## 🩺 How to Interpret Your BMI

- Use BMI as a general guideline, not as a medical diagnosis.
- Combine it with other health indicators such as waist circumference, lifestyle, and physical activity level.
- Consult a healthcare professional for a comprehensive evaluation.

> **Note:** BMI is a useful tool for gaining insight into your weight status, but it should always be viewed in context. Your overall health is influenced by many factors beyond BMI alone.
